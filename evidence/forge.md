# Crohnoz Forge · Product R&D Case

`L1 · PROTOTYPE / R&D`

**Discovery systems · assumptions · evidence · stage gates · local-first thinking**

</div>

> Forge is intentionally presented as an early product-system prototype. It demonstrates product reasoning and workflow design; it is **not** positioned as production software.

---

## Problem

Ideas often move into implementation before the problem, users, constraints, risks and success criteria are explicit. Forge explores how that reasoning can become a repeatable product workflow before expensive implementation decisions are locked in.

## What exists today

Forge combines two exploratory surfaces:

- a public workspace that converts a plain-language problem into a structured product blueprint;
- a local-first Studio concept organized around a lifecycle of `Raw → Discovery → Blueprint → Prototype → Testing → Outcome`.

The current evidence is strongest around **product-system framing**, not production operations.

## What it demonstrates

| Area | Current evidence |
|---|---|
| **Discovery** | Problem, users, constraints and assumptions are made explicit |
| **Evidence** | Decisions can be tied to evidence instead of remaining implicit |
| **Stage gates** | Readiness can be evaluated before moving deeper into implementation |
| **Privacy** | Local-first operation is treated as a product constraint |
| **Handoff** | Portable artifacts reduce dependency on a single workspace |

## Current maturity

Forge remains **L1 Prototype / R&D**. The next meaningful maturity gate is not more UI: it is validated use in a real product-discovery workflow, repeatable evidence of decision quality and clearer operational feedback from actual projects.

### What is not claimed

- production-grade multi-user operation;
- scaled commercial adoption;
- validated AI autonomy;
- production reliability or long-term operational metrics.

## Publication boundary

This public case exposes product reasoning, workflow concepts and safe demonstrations. Private project content, credentials, internal commercial processes and proprietary future services remain outside the public evidence layer.
