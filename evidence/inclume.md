# IncluMe · Inclusive Product R&D

`L1 · EARLY PRODUCT`

**Accessibility · citizen workflows · municipal review · geographic information · feedback loops**

</div>

> IncluMe is an early civic-product exploration. It demonstrates inclusive product framing and multi-stakeholder workflow design; it is **not** positioned as a production public-service platform.

---

## Problem

Accessibility information is often fragmented, outdated or difficult to verify. IncluMe explores a workflow for discovering, documenting and reviewing accessible parking information in Chile.

## What exists today

IncluMe separates two complementary public demonstrations:

- a citizen-facing experience for map-based discovery, search and community contribution;
- a municipal-facing surface for territorial review, indicators, submissions and export-oriented workflows.

The product evidence is strongest around **role separation and inclusive interaction design**.

## What it demonstrates

| Area | Current evidence |
|---|---|
| **Inclusive framing** | Accessibility is treated as the core domain rather than a decorative feature |
| **Citizen workflow** | Search, map discovery and correction/reporting are separated from institutional review |
| **Institutional workflow** | Municipal actors receive a distinct review-oriented surface |
| **Feedback loop** | Contribution, review and status form an explicit evidence cycle |
| **Geographic UX** | Territorial information is organized around user-oriented discovery |

## Current maturity

IncluMe remains **L1 Early Product**. The next maturity gate is validated use with representative users and institutional stakeholders, a maintained data-quality process and evidence that contribution/review loops work beyond a demonstration environment.

### What is not claimed

- authoritative national accessibility dataset;
- production municipal adoption;
- validated moderation at scale;
- complete geographic coverage;
- production SLA or long-term operational metrics.

## Publication boundary

The public demos can remain inspectable without exposing deployment credentials, moderation internals, sensitive contributor information, private datasets or proprietary service logic.
